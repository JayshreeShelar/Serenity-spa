About Serenity

Where Wellness Meets Tranquility

Serenity Spa was imagined as a quiet pause in a busy world. Every detail, from the soft light to the scent of fresh botanicals, is chosen to help you slow down and breathe.

No two guests are the same, so no two experiences are either. Each visit begins with a conversation about how you feel, and your treatment is shaped around what your body and mind need most, in peaceful surroundings that invite mindful self-care.
